Lyrics by Purnam Allahabadi and,
Performed by Ustad Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan

"Tumhein Dillagi Bhool Jaani Padegi” is among the most beloved qawwalis performed by Ustad Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan, known for its gentle melodic progression and emotionally restrained intensity. The composition unfolds as a conversation between longing and surrender, where devotion and human affection remain deliberately intertwined.

The verses sung in this qawwali draw from multiple poetic sources within the Urdu literary tradition. Some couplets are attributed to poets such as 'Jigar Moradabadi', while others belong to a shared oral repertoire that has travelled through performances and mehfils over time, reflecting the collective and evolving nature of qawwali.

( I couldn't understand the demand of your gaze,
