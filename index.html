About

Welcome to Qawwali Translations

Qawwali is not only sound, but language, metaphor, and devotion This space is a small attempt to listen closely to the poetry of qawwali — to the words sung by Ustad Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan, and to the meanings they carry. Here, selected kalaams are presented with their transliterations and translations, preserving poetic flow, cultural context, and the spirit in which they are sung.
