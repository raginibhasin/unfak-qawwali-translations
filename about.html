About This Project

This project exists as a quiet space for engaging with qawwali poetry beyond sound alone. While qawwali is primarily experienced through performance, its lyrics carry layers of emotion, devotion, longing, and spiritual inquiry that often go unnoticed across language barriers.

The translations and transliterations presented here are intended to preserve poetic sense rather than offer strictly literal renderings. Wherever possible, care has been taken to maintain tone, flow, and emotional weight, allowing readers to approach the lyrics with both understanding and openness.

This site focuses on selected works performed by Ustad Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan, whose musical legacy continues to shape the global understanding of qawwali. Lyricists are credited where known, and all original compositions remain the intellectual property of their respective creators.

This project is non-commercial and created for educational and devotional appreciation.
