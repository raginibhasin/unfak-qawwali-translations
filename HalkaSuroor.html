Yeh Jo Halka Halka Suroor Hai

By Ustad Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan

Lyrics and Translations

"Yeh Jo Halka Halka Suroor Hai"

"یہ جو ہلکا ہلکا سرور ہے"

( this faint-delicate intoxication that surrounds me )

Meaning and Interpretation of the Lyrics:

Detailed Interpretation : The word “Suroor” means a gentle state of bliss or intoxication — not necessarily from wine, but from overwhelming emotion or divine ecstasy. The poet is describing how he feels light-headed, almost spiritually drunk, as if under a sweet, mysterious spell. It’s a metaphor for love’s influence — a state where one loses control over their senses, not because of alcohol, but because of the overwhelming beauty or presence of the beloved (which can be a human lover, or in Sufi symbolism, God Himself).

"Yeh Teri Nazar Ka Kasoor Hai"

"یہ تیری نظر کا قصور ہے"

( this the fault of your gaze )

Meaning and Interpretation of the Lyrics:

Detailed Interpretation : The speaker immediately assigns the cause of their 'Suroor'. It's not wine or any outside substance; it's the mesmerizing, captivating power of the beloved's eyes. The word 'Kasoor' (Fault) is used playfully, as a way of admiring the profound effect the beloved has. In Sufi literature, the “Nazar” (Divine Glance or Gaze) is known to transform and awaken the lover’s soul. One look from the Beloved is enough to set the heart ablaze.
